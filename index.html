Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Sweet Curls and your curl transformation journey

What is Sweet Curls and how does it work?

Sweet Curls is an innovative hydrolyzed Naticol collagen powder specifically formulated for wavy and curly hair. It works from the inside out — when you drink it daily, the collagen peptides, vitamins, and active ingredients are absorbed into your bloodstream and delivered directly to your hair follicles. Over time, this helps rebuild hair structure, improve elasticity, and enhance your natural curl pattern from bulbs to ends.

How long until I see results?

Most customers begin to notice visible improvements within 2 to 4 weeks of consistent daily use. For the best and most lasting results, we recommend completing the full 30-day treatment cycle. Hair grows slowly, so continued use over multiple cycles will deliver even more dramatic transformation in curl definition, shine, and overall hair health.

Is Sweet Curls suitable for all hair types?

Sweet Curls is specifically designed for wavy, curly, and coily hair types. However, the nourishing ingredients — collagen, hyaluronic acid, biotin, and zinc — benefit all hair types by improving hydration, strength, and overall hair health. If you have curly hair and want more defined, bouncy, and healthier curls, this product is made for you!

When is the best time to take Sweet Curls?

You can take Sweet Curls anytime that fits your routine — morning, afternoon, or evening. Many customers prefer taking it in the morning as part of their daily ritual, or before bed when the body naturally repairs and regenerates. The most important thing is consistency — take it every day for the best results.

Can I take Sweet Curls with other supplements?

Sweet Curls is formulated to be a complete hair nutrition solution on its own. If you're already taking other supplements, we recommend checking with your healthcare provider to avoid exceeding the recommended daily intake of certain vitamins and minerals, especially zinc and vitamin C, which are already included in Sweet Curls.

What does Sweet Curls taste like?

Sweet Curls has a delightful peony and lavender flavor — delicate, floral, and refreshing. It mixes easily with 150ml of water to create a pleasant-tasting drink that you'll actually look forward to every day. No fishy aftertaste, just a light and enjoyable daily ritual.
